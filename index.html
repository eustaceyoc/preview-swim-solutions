Winter 2026–27 outlook

A wetter winter may be on the way.

NOAA has an El Niño Advisory in place, and the National Weather Service in Fort Worth says its North Texas outlook leans toward above-normal precipitation this winter. That’s an outlook, not a promise, but heavy rain is hard on pool water.

Sources: NOAA Climate Prediction Center, 12 Nov 2026 · NWS Fort Worth winter outlook

Rain throws off chemistryHeavy rain dilutes sanitizer and shifts pH, so water should be tested after a storm.
Debris loads the filterLeaves and runoff end up in skimmers, baskets and the filter.
Equipment works harderWhen a pump or filter is already struggling, a wet stretch often makes it obvious.
