# Swim Solutions Pools, Inc: sample site

A one-page sample site I put together for Swim Solutions Pools, Inc (Dallas, TX). It's a concept, not their official website, and it says so at the top of the page and in the footer.

Live preview: https://preview-swim-solutions.vercel.app

The request form doesn't send anything to a server. It just opens your own email app with a message addressed to info@swimsolutionspools.com, and the phone buttons dial (214) 932-9131. There's no tracking or analytics, and the fonts are self-hosted.

## What's on the page

Only details from Swim Solutions Pools, Inc's own website and public listings: business name, Dallas, phone, email, P.O. Box, hours, and the residential, commercial, repair and remodeling services listed on swimsolutionspools.com. The winter section cites NOAA and the NWS Fort Worth outlook. Anything I couldn't confirm is left out or marked to be confirmed. There are no reviews, ratings, prices, years in business or team photos.

## Indexing

The page has `noindex, nofollow` in a meta tag and in the `X-Robots-Tag` header (`vercel.json`), and `robots.txt` disallows everything, so it shouldn't turn up in search.
